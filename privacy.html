This website is operated by Hendrik Schulte-Bahrenberg, a SAP EWM consultant based in Germany. If you have any questions about how your data is handled, you can reach out via LinkedIn.

2. What data is collected and why

This website collects data in two ways:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — only if you accept cookies. GA4 collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time on page, and approximate geographic region. This data is used solely to understand which content is useful to readers. No personal profiles are built and no data is sold to third parties.

3. Cookies

This website uses a single cookie consent mechanism. If you decline cookies, no tracking cookies are set and Google Analytics is not loaded. Your choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so you are not asked again on future visits.

If you accepted cookies and want to withdraw consent, you can clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time.

4. Google Analytics

If you accept cookies, this site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google LLC. Data is transmitted to Google servers, which may be located in the United States. Google processes this data according to their own Privacy Policy. IP anonymization is enabled.

By subscribing you agree to your email address being stored and processed by Mailchimp for the purpose of sending you newsletter emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.
